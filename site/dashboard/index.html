What I'd do with this data

Reading the numbers is the job; acting on them is the value.

01

Recalibrate the lead score

B-grades earn their rank, but D-grades out-collected C-grades roughly 2:1 — the model is inverting the middle of the list. Retrain it on cash collected, not survey completeness, and re-route call-slots accordingly.

02

Treat email as the closer, not a channel

The email list books few calls but they close at a dramatically higher rate than paid traffic. Push webinar registrants into the nurture list faster — the second touch is where the money is.

03

Fix Facebook or feed it less

Facebook delivered ~23% of leads and ~3% of cash. Before killing it: split creative fatigue from audience quality by running IG's top 3 creatives on FB placements only. If it still won't close, reallocate.

04

Mind the payment-plan exposure

Most buyers are on plans or deposits, so collected ≠ contracted. Track expected vs collected per cohort and chase the deposit→PIF conversion — that's silent revenue already sold.

05

Capital sweet spot: $1K–$3K

That bracket produced the most closes and the most cash. The "0 savings" segment is huge (2,500+ leads) but converts thin — qualify harder pre-call and give closers the capital answer up front.

06

Dedupe at capture

38 duplicate emails and 135 duplicate phones made it into the sheet. Small fix (normalize + upsert at the form), real effect: cleaner attribution and no double-booked calls.

Metrics I would add

What this dashboard can't show yet — and exactly what it needs to show it.

CPL / CAC / ROAS per creative

Cash per campaign is half the picture — spend is the other half. The leaderboard is already keyed by campaign, so this is a join, not a rebuild.

Needs: Meta Ads spend export (campaign-level, daily).

Show-up rate

Registration → live attendance is THE webinar metric; every insight above sharpens once attendance is known per source and creative.

Needs: Zoom attendance export with emails (hashed is fine).

Speed-to-lead

Minutes from opt-in to first outreach vs booked-rate. Usually the single cheapest conversion win in a call funnel.

Needs: first-touch timestamps from Close.

Refund & delinquency tracking

A Refunds pipeline exists in the CRM but isn't in this export. Payment-plan funnels live or die on net revenue, not gross collected.

Needs: refunds + missed-installment feed.

LTV by cohort

Course ($4K) vs Full Program ($7K) buyers by source and month — which channel buys upgrades, not just entries.

Needs: post-purchase transaction history.

Creative fatigue curves

Cash-per-lead by creative by week — catch "Dumbest Thing" declining before spend does. The date + campaign keys already exist in this dataset.

Needs: nothing — next iteration of this page.
