Deliverable 01 · Landing Page

One proven funnel. Three design hypotheses.

Same converting copy, same structure, same registration flow — three distinct visual directions built to be split-tested, not debated. Every variant ships with a guided design-rationale tour (the gold button, bottom-left) explaining the marketing decision behind each section, and every form hands off to the real confirmation flow tagged with its funnelVersion.

Variant 01

How I'd run this: ship all three behind the same ad traffic at 33/33/33 for one webinar cycle.
