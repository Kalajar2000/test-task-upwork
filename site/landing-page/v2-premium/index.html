The wallet he refused to keep

Three jobs. Restaurant floor at €11 an hour, bakery shifts on weekends, code at night. A first-generation kid in France whose only way up was the internet — and every internet model seemed to come with a catch.

Dropshipping? Quit the day he looked hard at what he was selling. Crypto? It worked — that was the problem. He deleted the wallet and binned the Ledger with his gains still on it, and started over with one rule: if it isn't clean, it doesn't count.

That rule cost him six months. It became the entire brand.

The model that didn't need a catch

The answer turned out to be boring and beautiful: brands need influencers, influencers need deals, and someone has to broker the middle — over email, for a fee. No inventory. No audience. No grey areas.

He ran that desk from a laptop until it paid $30K a month — then taught it to 17 brothers in a free group. The group became a mentorship. The mentorship became a company: 55 people across 7 countries, and students who've quit their 9-5s and rebuilt their families on income they never have to explain away.

The honest answers

Is the money actually halal? +

Yes. The fee is payment for real brokerage work between brands and influencers. No interest, no gambling, no products you'd have to hide. The halal audit is the first thing taught on the class — and it's the reason this model exists at all.

I'm starting from zero. Is that a problem? +

The class assumes zero. No audience, no capital, no tech background. If you can write a clear email, the mechanics are learnable — the roadmap segment covers exactly what to learn first.

I already work full-time. +

So did most students when they started. The work stacks into evenings and weekends — 2–3 focused hours a day is the honest ask. Nobody quits a paycheck on day one; the point is to make quitting optional.

Do I need money to start? +

No stock, no ads, no storefront. A laptop and consistency. This is a skill business, not a capital business.

How is this different from e-com, FBA or trading? +

There's nothing to buy, hold or gamble. You're paid for work, not for exposure to risk. A lot of students arrived here after one of those models — this is the one that stuck.

What does the free class cost later? +

Nothing. The masterclass stands on its own. A paid program exists for people who want implementation help — he mentions it once, at the end, and that's that.

What if I can't make it live? +

Come live. Seats are capped, questions get answered in the room, and a replay is never promised. If it matters, make the 90 minutes.
